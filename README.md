# Isotherm Applied Sciences Inc.

Isotherm Applied Sciences is an independent engineering studio focused on building deterministic, transparent, and minimal‑systems software. Our work centers on identity, provenance, verification, and continuity tools designed for collectors, archivists, institutions, and developers.

We build small, reliable systems that do one thing exceptionally well — and integrate cleanly into larger workflows.

---

## Mission

To create open‑source tools that strengthen trust, authenticity, and continuity across digital and physical ecosystems. Our philosophy emphasizes clarity, auditability, and deterministic behavior.

---

## Focus Areas

- Receipt and provenance verification  
- Identity continuity systems  
- Local‑first processing  
- Minimal, deterministic architectures  
- Developer‑friendly open‑source tooling
